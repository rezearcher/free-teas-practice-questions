## The questions

### Reading

**1. Read the passage: "Regular physical activity strengthens the heart, improves circulation, and helps the body regulate blood sugar. Even a brisk daily walk can lower the risk of several chronic diseases." Which statement best expresses the main idea?**

- A. Physical activity provides broad benefits for health. ✅
- B. Walking is the only form of exercise worth doing.
- C. Chronic disease cannot be prevented.
- D. Circulation has nothing to do with the heart.

> **Answer: A.** The passage lists several benefits of physical activity, so the main idea is that it provides broad health benefits. The other choices overstate, contradict, or are unsupported by the text.

**2. A notice reads: "The clinic will be closed Monday for staff training. Please schedule appointments for Tuesday or later." What is the author's primary purpose?**

- A. To persuade readers to change clinics
- B. To inform readers of a closure and what to do ✅
- C. To entertain readers with a story
- D. To criticize the training program

> **Answer: B.** The notice states a fact (a closure) and gives a direction (schedule later), so its purpose is to inform. There is no persuasion, narrative, or criticism.

**3. Which of the following is a statement of fact rather than opinion?**

- A. The new wing of the hospital is the most beautiful building in town.
- B. Nurses have the hardest job of anyone.
- C. The hospital has 240 licensed beds. ✅
- D. Everyone should become a nurse.

> **Answer: C.** A fact can be verified. The number of licensed beds is verifiable, while 'most beautiful,' 'hardest,' and 'should' express opinions or judgments.

**4. Read the passage: "First, gather the equipment. Next, wash your hands. Then put on gloves. Finally, open the sterile package." What text structure does the author use?**

- A. Compare and contrast
- B. Cause and effect
- C. Problem and solution
- D. Sequence (chronological order) ✅

> **Answer: D.** Signal words 'first,' 'next,' 'then,' and 'finally' show steps in order, which is a sequence or chronological structure.

**5. Passage: "By the time Maria reached the pharmacy counter, her hands were shaking and she could barely read the label." What can the reader most reasonably infer?**

- A. Maria is experiencing stress or physical distress. ✅
- B. Maria is a pharmacist.
- C. The pharmacy was closed.
- D. The label was written in another language.

> **Answer: A.** Shaking hands and difficulty reading suggest stress or physical distress. The other options add details the passage does not support.

### Mathematics

**1. Evaluate: 24 - 6 × (8 - 5) + 2**

- A. 56
- B. 20
- C. 8 ✅
- D. 14

> **Answer: C.** Using order of operations: 8 - 5 = 3, then 6 × 3 = 18, then 24 - 18 + 2 = 8.

**2. A patient is to receive 30% of a 500 mL bag over the first hour. How many milliliters is that?**

- A. 120 mL
- B. 170 mL
- C. 200 mL
- D. 150 mL ✅

> **Answer: D.** 30% of 500 = 0.30 × 500 = 150 mL.

**3. What is 3/4 + 1/6, expressed as a single fraction in lowest terms?**

- A. 11/12 ✅
- B. 4/10
- C. 5/6
- D. 7/12

> **Answer: A.** Use a common denominator of 12: 3/4 = 9/12 and 1/6 = 2/12, so 9/12 + 2/12 = 11/12, already in lowest terms.

**4. If 4 ounces of solution contain 12 milligrams of medication, how many milligrams are in 10 ounces at the same concentration?**

- A. 24 mg
- B. 30 mg ✅
- C. 36 mg
- D. 40 mg

> **Answer: B.** Set up a proportion: 12/4 = x/10, so x = 10 × (12/4) = 10 × 3 = 30 mg.

**5. Solve for x: 3x - 7 = 20**

- A. 7
- B. 4
- C. 9 ✅
- D. 13

> **Answer: C.** Add 7 to both sides: 3x = 27. Divide by 3: x = 9.

### Science

**1. What is the primary function of red blood cells?**

- A. To carry oxygen to body tissues ✅
- B. To fight infection
- C. To form blood clots
- D. To produce antibodies

> **Answer: A.** Red blood cells contain hemoglobin, which binds and transports oxygen to tissues. Fighting infection and producing antibodies are roles of white blood cells; clotting involves platelets.

**2. The heart is the central organ of which body system?**

- A. Respiratory system
- B. Cardiovascular (circulatory) system ✅
- C. Digestive system
- D. Nervous system

> **Answer: B.** The heart pumps blood through vessels, making it the central organ of the cardiovascular (circulatory) system.

**3. Which cell organelle is primarily responsible for producing energy (ATP)?**

- A. Nucleus
- B. Ribosome
- C. Mitochondrion ✅
- D. Golgi apparatus

> **Answer: C.** The mitochondrion generates most of a cell's ATP through cellular respiration, which is why it is often called the powerhouse of the cell.

**4. On the pH scale, a solution with a pH of 3 is best described as:**

- A. strongly basic
- B. neutral
- C. the same as pure water
- D. acidic ✅

> **Answer: D.** The pH scale runs from 0 to 14; values below 7 are acidic. A pH of 3 is acidic. Pure water is neutral at pH 7.

**5. Which of the following is a primary function of the skeletal system?**

- A. Providing structural support and protecting organs ✅
- B. Producing digestive enzymes
- C. Filtering the blood
- D. Regulating body temperature through sweat

> **Answer: A.** The skeletal system supports the body and protects internal organs (for example, the skull protects the brain). The other functions belong to the digestive, urinary, and integumentary systems.

### English & Language Usage

**1. Choose the sentence with correct subject-verb agreement.**

- A. The list of supplies are on the counter.
- B. The list of supplies were on the counter.
- C. The list of supplies is on the counter. ✅
- D. The list of supplies have been on the counter.

> **Answer: C.** The subject is 'list' (singular); 'of supplies' is a prepositional phrase that does not change the verb. The singular verb 'is' agrees with 'list.'

**2. Which sentence is punctuated correctly?**

- A. After the exam we celebrated, with pizza and music.
- B. After the exam we celebrated with pizza, and music.
- C. After, the exam we celebrated with pizza and music.
- D. After the exam, we celebrated with pizza and music. ✅

> **Answer: D.** A comma follows the introductory phrase 'After the exam,' and no other comma is needed. The other options misplace the comma.

**3. Choose the sentence that uses the correct pronoun.**

- A. The instructor gave the assignment to Jordan and me. ✅
- B. The instructor gave the assignment to Jordan and I.
- C. The instructor gave the assignment to I and Jordan.
- D. The instructor gave the assignment to myself and Jordan.

> **Answer: A.** As the object of the preposition 'to,' the objective pronoun 'me' is correct. Testing each pronoun alone ('to me') confirms it.

**4. Choose the sentence that uses the correct word.**

- A. The new policy will effect every department.
- B. The new policy will affect every department. ✅
- C. The new policy will have an affect on every department.
- D. The new policy will effected every department.

> **Answer: B.** 'Affect' is usually the verb meaning to influence; 'effect' is usually the noun meaning a result. The policy will 'affect' (influence) the departments.

**5. Which sentence is capitalized correctly?**

- A. We visited the Pacific ocean last Summer.
- B. We visited the pacific Ocean last summer.
- C. We visited the Pacific Ocean last summer. ✅
- D. we visited the Pacific Ocean last Summer.

> **Answer: C.** 'Pacific Ocean' is a proper noun and is capitalized; the season 'summer' is not capitalized unless it begins a sentence.
